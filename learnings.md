## What works
- **Eliminate hot-loop stack arrays and consume values directly from source data or short-lived locals.** This is the strongest recurring win in `diffuse.c` (`strip-internal-array-allocations-for-find_gradients-normalizations` +11.9%, `inline-symmetric-convolutions` +11.7%, `compound-near-miss-optimizations` +6.2%).
- **Use algebraic rewrites that reduce intermediate state without changing the overall loop structure.** Good examples are direct symmetric convolution algebra, direct angular-term derivation from existing squares, anisotropic decomposition into isotropic base plus directional correction, and direct isotropic inlining (`compute-cos2-sin2-cos-sin-directly` +2.3%, `decompose-anisotropic-to-isotropic-base-and-correction` +1.7%, `inline-isotropic-convolutions` +1.2%).
- **Apply outer-loop unswitching only when it deletes expensive per-pixel math or whole passes.** This paid off when it removed angle/sqrt/exp work or skipped negligible scales entirely (`outer-loop-unswitch-isotropic-math` +6.4%, `skip-hf-decomposition-for-negligible-scales` +5.0%, `outer-unswitch-zero-speed-order-pairs` +2.8%, `skip-pde-negligible-norm` +2.0%).
- **Pre-combine matched LF/HF work only when it removes an entire duplicated convolution.** The successful case was combining matched stencil sums before evaluation, not just shaving a few scalar ops (`precombine-stencil-sums-for-matched-orders` +3.0%).
- **Use nontemporal stores only for write-once buffers with long reuse distance.** That helped for HF detail writes and final output writes, where cache pollution mattered more than short-term reuse (`nontemporal-hf-writes-in-decomposition` +7.3%, `nontemporal-output-stores` +1.3%).
- **Peel boundary handling out of the hottest PDE column loop when it creates a large unclamped center region without changing traversal order.** That specific clamp-removal pattern was worthwhile (`peel-column-loop-to-eliminate-clamping` +2.4%).

## What to avoid
- **Do not inline or scalarize the `dt_vector_exp` path or force vector temporaries into scalar form.** Those attempts caused catastrophic regressions (`inline-expf-evaluation-anisotropy` -170.9%, `inline-scalar-expf-fuse-all-pixel-passes` -114.4%, `explicit-scalarization-of-accumulation-arrays` -111.8%).
- **Do not fuse many hot `for_each_channel` phases into one giant pixel body.** The saved loop overhead is consistently outweighed by register pressure and worse codegen (`pair-4-compute_convolution-calls-into-2-for_each_channel-loops` -48.7%, `phase-gradient-laplacian-into-scoped-blocks` -4.0%, `fuse-variance-regularization-into-loading-loop` -8.2%).
- **Do not disturb the established memory traversal pattern.** Prefetching, row-order changes, cache blocking, bounds unswitching for row access, and LF/HF load interleaving repeatedly regressed (`remove-dwt-interleave-rows-to-restore-sequential-prefetching` -30.1%, `unswitch-row-bounds-for-static-offsets` -26.7%, `cache-block-pde-column-strips` -12.4%, `prefetch-interleaved-row-neighbors` -6.0%, `interleave-lf-hf-neighbor-loads` -3.5%).
- **Do not use the wrong store policy for short-reuse buffers.** Streaming LF writes and temporalizing intermediate PDE outputs both lost badly; reuse distance dominates theory here (`nontemporal-lf-writes-in-decomposition` -68.8%, `temporal-stores-for-intermediate-pde-scales` -6.0%, `normal-stores-intermediate-iteration-output` -0.3%).
- **Avoid outer-loop specialization when it only removes tiny work.** Variants for zero sharpness, zero LF contribution, matched anisotropy, or fewer dispatch instantiations added code-size and scheduling cost without enough savings (`outer-unswitch-zero-lf-contribution` -15.4%, `outer-unswitch-zero-sharpness` -13.1%, `outer-loop-unswitch-matched-anisotropy` -17.1%, `reduce-dispatch-instantiations-16-to-9` -0.1%).
- **Avoid aggressive convolution fusion beyond the simple matched-stencil-sum case.** More ambitious attempts to merge matched tensors, combine isotropic orders, or pair whole convolution passes were neutral to disastrous (`fuse-matched-anisotropic-tensors` -7.1%, `fuse-matched-gradient-and-laplacian-accumulations` -0.1%, `combine-isotropic-abcd-coefficients` -0.1%, `pair-4-compute_convolution-calls-into-2-for_each_channel-loops` -48.7%).
- **Avoid “fast-math” style micro-ALU rewrites.** Reciprocal/division swaps, rsqrt paths, explicit FMA chains, and small algebraic reshufflings were usually noise or regressions (`replace-angle-divisions-with-inverse-magnitude` -2.6%, `rsqrt-anisotropic-magnitude` -0.8%, `explicit-fmaf-variance-chain` -2.2%, `extract-inverse-c2-subexpression` -4.4%, `precompute-directional-correction-factor` -2.2%).
- **Do not prioritize setup/allocation/threading overhead changes unless profiling proves they matter.** Most such work was neutral or negative because the runtime is dominated by the pixel loops (`defer-unused-image-buffer-allocations` ~0%, `free-hf-buffers-after-reconstruction` -0.1%, `precompute-scale-parameters-outside-iteration-loop` -0.4%, `hoist-omp-parallel-across-scales` -2.7%, `allocate-contiguous-hf-buffer-pool` -5.1%).
