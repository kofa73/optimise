## What works
- **Eliminate hot-loop stack arrays and read directly from source data or short-lived locals.** This is the strongest repeated win in `diffuse.c` (`strip-internal-array-allocations-for-find_gradients-normalizations` +11.9%, `inline-symmetric-convolutions` +11.7%, `compound-near-miss-optimizations` +6.2%).
- **Use algebraic rewrites that reduce intermediate state without changing the overall loop shape.** Successful examples include direct symmetric convolution algebra, anisotropic decomposition into isotropic base plus directional correction, and direct computation of angular terms from already-available squares (`decompose-anisotropic-to-isotropic-base-and-correction` +1.7%, `compute-cos2-sin2-cos-sin-directly` +2.3%, `inline-isotropic-convolutions` +1.2%).
- **Apply outer-loop unswitching only when it removes genuinely expensive per-pixel work.** Skipping angle/sqrt/exp math or whole negligible passes paid off repeatedly (`outer-loop-unswitch-isotropic-math` +6.4%, `skip-hf-decomposition-for-negligible-scales` +5.0%, `outer-unswitch-zero-speed-order-pairs` +2.8%, `skip-pde-negligible-norm` +2.0%).
- **Use nontemporal stores only for buffers that are written once and not reused until much later.** That helped both HF decomposition writes and final output writes (`nontemporal-hf-writes-in-decomposition` +7.3%, `nontemporal-output-stores` +1.3%).
- **Pre-combine matched work when it removes an entire duplicated convolution, not just a couple of ALU ops.** The best example is combining matched LF/HF stencil sums before convolution (`precombine-stencil-sums-for-matched-orders` +3.0%).
- **Peel boundary handling out of the hottest PDE column loop when it produces a large unclamped center region without disturbing traversal order.** That specific change was worthwhile (`peel-column-loop-to-eliminate-clamping` +2.4%).

## What to avoid
- **Do not break the compiler's SIMD strategy around `dt_vector_exp` or manually scalarize vector state.** Inlining/scalarizing exp or accumulation state caused catastrophic regressions (`inline-expf-evaluation-anisotropy` -170.9%, `inline-scalar-expf-fuse-all-pixel-passes` -114.4%, `explicit-scalarization-of-accumulation-arrays` -111.8%).
- **Do not fuse multiple hot `for_each_channel` phases into one giant body.** The extra register pressure consistently outweighed saved loop overhead (`pair-4-compute_convolution-calls-into-2-for_each_channel-loops` -48.7%, `phase-gradient-laplacian-into-scoped-blocks` -4.0%, `fuse-variance-regularization-into-loading-loop` -8.2%).
- **Avoid inner-loop branching or runtime-dispatched skipping, even when the condition is loop-invariant.** Skipping zero-speed work, mask checks, matched copies, or sparse-mask branches inside the hot path hurt badly (`skip-zero-speed-orders` -82.4%, `copy-matched-anisotropy-c2-to-skip-exp-results` +0.3% at best, `mask-run-spans` -7.8%).
- **Do not disturb the established memory traversal pattern.** Software prefetching, load interleaving, cache blocking, row-order changes, and similar access-pattern rewrites repeatedly regressed (`remove-dwt-interleave-rows-to-restore-sequential-prefetching` -30.1%, `unswitch-row-bounds-for-static-offsets` -26.7%, `cache-block-pde-column-strips` -12.4%, `prefetch-interleaved-row-neighbors` -6.0%, `interleave-lf-hf-neighbor-loads` -3.5%).
- **Do not use the wrong store policy for short-reuse buffers.** Streaming LF writes or trying to keep intermediate PDE outputs temporal both regressed; reuse distance matters more than theory (`nontemporal-lf-writes-in-decomposition` -68.8%, `temporal-stores-for-intermediate-pde-scales` -6.0%, `normal-stores-intermediate-iteration-output` -0.3%).
- **Avoid outer-loop specialization for tiny savings.** Unswitching to remove a single multiply, a small load group, or similar light work tended to bloat code and lose (`outer-unswitch-zero-lf-contribution` -15.4%, `outer-unswitch-zero-sharpness` -13.1%, `outer-loop-unswitch-matched-anisotropy` -17.1%).
- **Avoid hand “fast-math” rewrites and micro-ALU tweaks.** Reciprocal/division rewrites, rsqrt paths, explicit FMA chains, and small algebraic reshufflings were usually neutral or negative (`replace-angle-divisions-with-inverse-magnitude` -2.6%, `rsqrt-anisotropic-magnitude` -0.8%, `explicit-fmaf-variance-chain` -2.2%, `extract-inverse-c2-subexpression` -4.4%, `precompute-directional-correction-factor` -2.2%).
- **Do not spend effort on setup/allocation/parallel-region overhead unless profiling proves it matters.** Most such changes were noise or regressions because the runtime is dominated by the pixel loops (`defer-unused-image-buffer-allocations` ~0%, `free-hf-buffers-after-reconstruction` -0.1%, `precompute-scale-parameters-outside-iteration-loop` -0.4%, `hoist-omp-parallel-across-scales` -2.7%, `allocate-contiguous-hf-buffer-pool` -5.1%).
