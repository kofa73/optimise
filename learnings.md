## What works
- **Eliminate hot-loop stack arrays and materialized intermediate kernels/sums; consume source pixels or short-lived locals directly.** This is still the strongest recurring win in `diffuse.c` (`strip-internal-array-allocations-for-find_gradients-normalizations` +11.9%, `inline-symmetric-convolutions` +11.7%, `compound-near-miss-optimizations` +6.2%).
- **Prefer algebraic rewrites that reuse already-computed values and reduce live state without changing traversal order.** Good examples are deriving angular terms from existing squares, removing canceled `0.5f` factors, decomposing anisotropic accumulation into isotropic base plus directional correction, and directly inlining the isotropic convolution (`compute-cos2-sin2-cos-sin-directly` +2.3%, `remove-0.5-scaling-from-gradient-laplacian-computation` +1.5%, `decompose-anisotropic-to-isotropic-base-and-correction` +1.7%, `inline-isotropic-convolutions` +1.2%).
- **Specialize outside the hot loop only when it removes substantial per-pixel math or skips whole passes/scales.** This works when it deletes angle/sqrt/exp work or bypasses negligible-scale processing (`outer-loop-unswitch-isotropic-math` +6.4%, `skip-hf-decomposition-for-negligible-scales` +5.0%, `outer-unswitch-zero-speed-order-pairs` +2.8%, `skip-pde-negligible-norm` +2.0%).
- **Preset-shaped fast paths can pay when they bypass a large chunk of generic mixed-mode plumbing instead of just shaving a few ops.** The clear example is the no-mask all-isophote helper (`all-isophote-pde-fastpath` +10.8%).
- **Fuse matched LF/HF work only at the stencil-sum level when it truly removes duplicate convolution evaluation.** The proven case is precombining matched stencil sums before convolution, not broader tensor fusion (`precombine-stencil-sums-for-matched-orders` +3.0%).
- **Use nontemporal stores only for write-once buffers with long reuse distance.** This paid off for HF detail writes and final output writes, where avoiding cache pollution mattered more than locality (`nontemporal-hf-writes-in-decomposition` +7.3%, `nontemporal-output-stores` +1.3%).
- **Peel only the hot PDE column loop to expose a large unclamped center region while keeping the established traversal order.** That specific clamp-removal pattern was worthwhile (`peel-column-loop-to-eliminate-clamping` +2.4%).
- **For masked presets, the worthwhile ROI work is the kind that deletes repeated whole-frame processing, not just mask bookkeeping.** The successful masked optimization was scale-aware ROI cropping (`masked-scale-roi` about +5.8%).

## What to avoid
- **Do not scalarize or inline the `dt_vector_exp` path, and do not force vector temporaries into scalar form.** Those caused the worst regressions in the log (`inline-expf-evaluation-anisotropy` -170.9%, `inline-scalar-expf-fuse-all-pixel-passes` -114.4%, `explicit-scalarization-of-accumulation-arrays` -111.8%).
- **Do not fuse many hot `for_each_channel` phases into one giant pixel body, and do not add new derived intermediates just to save a later loop.** The small loop-overhead savings were repeatedly crushed by register pressure and worse codegen (`pair-4-compute_convolution-calls-into-2-for_each_channel-loops` -48.7%, `phase-gradient-laplacian-into-scoped-blocks` -4.0%, `fuse-variance-regularization-into-loading-loop` -8.2%, `precompute-laplacian-and-axial-difference` -16.2%).
- **Do not disturb the established traversal and memory-scheduling pattern.** Row-order changes, cache blocking, prefetching, lookup tables, neighbor tables, and LF/HF load interleaving repeatedly regressed (`remove-dwt-interleave-rows-to-restore-sequential-prefetching` -30.1%, `unswitch-row-bounds-for-static-offsets` -26.7%, `cache-block-pde-column-strips` -12.4%, `prefetch-interleaved-row-neighbors` -6.0%, `interleave-lf-hf-neighbor-loads` -3.5%, `precompute-row-interleave-order` -3.0%, `pde-neighbor-index-tables` -3.3%).
- **Do not change store policy for short- or medium-reuse buffers based on theory alone.** Actual reuse distance dominates; the wrong store choice regressed hard (`nontemporal-lf-writes-in-decomposition` -68.8%, `temporal-stores-for-intermediate-pde-scales` -6.0%, `normal-stores-intermediate-iteration-output` -0.3%).
- **Avoid outer-loop specialization, helper proliferation, or dispatch expansion when it only removes tiny work.** These usually add code-size and scheduling cost without enough payoff (`outer-loop-unswitch-matched-anisotropy` -17.1%, `outer-unswitch-zero-lf-contribution` -15.4%, `outer-unswitch-zero-sharpness` -13.1%, `unit-variance-fastpath` -6.4%, `outer-unswitch-has-mask` -3.5%, `reduce-dispatch-instantiations-16-to-9` -0.1%, `fourth-order-solo-path` +0.4%).
- **Avoid aggressive tensor/convolution fusion beyond the simple matched-stencil-sum case.** More ambitious attempts to merge tensors, reuse kernels, combine isotropic orders, or rewrite the whole accumulation algebra were neutral to catastrophic (`fuse-matched-anisotropic-tensors` -7.1%, `algebraically-simplify-anisotropic-accumulation` -7.3%, `matched-order-kernel-reuse` -108.0%, `fuse-matched-gradient-and-laplacian-accumulations` -0.1%, `combine-isotropic-abcd-coefficients` -0.1%).
- **Avoid fast-math-style micro-ALU rewrites and tiny arithmetic substitutions.** Reciprocal/division swaps, rsqrt paths, explicit FMA chains, and similar local rewrites were usually noise or regressions (`replace-angle-divisions-with-inverse-magnitude` -2.6%, `rsqrt-anisotropic-magnitude` -0.8%, `explicit-fmaf-variance-chain` -2.2%, `extract-inverse-c2-subexpression` -4.4%, `precompute-directional-correction-factor` -2.2%, `precompute-reciprocal-variance` -3.1%).
- **Avoid mask-specific restructures unless they delete substantial whole-frame work.** Spans, per-row intervals, split base-plus-delta passes, and sparse init tweaks were neutral or negative once their extra control flow and passes were included (`mask-run-spans` -7.8%, `diffuse-mask-row-roi` -0.1%, `baseline-plus-delta` +0.2%, `sparse-inpaint-initialization` +0.5%, `skip-empty-coarse-mask-scales` +0.1%).
- **Do not prioritize setup, allocation, descriptor-building, or threading cleanups unless profiling shows they matter.** Most such changes were neutral or negative because runtime is dominated by the pixel loops (`defer-unused-image-buffer-allocations` ~0%, `free-hf-buffers-after-reconstruction` -0.1%, `precompute-scale-parameters-outside-iteration-loop` -0.4%, `prebuilt-scale-runtime-descriptors` -0.1%, `hoist-omp-parallel-across-scales` -2.7%, `allocate-contiguous-hf-buffer-pool` -5.1%).
