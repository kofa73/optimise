## What works
- **Start with hot-loop stack-array elimination and direct algebraic accumulation.** This is still the most reliable win source: remove materialized intermediates, keep values in registers, and use direct convolution algebra (`strip-internal-array-allocations-for-find_gradients-normalizations` +11.9%, `inline-symmetric-convolutions` +11.7%, `compound-near-miss-optimizations` +6.2%).
- **Prefer small algebraic simplifications that reduce live state without changing traversal.** Good examples are direct derivative-angle formulas and tensor simplifications, not broad loop rewrites (`compute-cos2-sin2-cos-sin-directly` +2.3%, `remove-0.5f-scaling-from-gradient-laplacian-computation` +1.5%, `decompose-anisotropic-to-isotropic-base-and-correction` +1.7%, `inline-isotropic-convolutions` +1.2%).
- **Outer-loop specialization pays when it removes expensive invariant work from every pixel or skips whole passes.** The successful cases deleted angle/sqrt/exp work or entire negligible-scale processing (`outer-loop-unswitch-isotropic-math` +6.4%, `skip-hf-decomposition-for-negligible-scales` +5.0%, `outer-unswitch-zero-speed-order-pairs` +2.8%, `skip-pde-negligible-norm` +2.0%).
- **Deleting whole-image or whole-scale work beats shaving a few ALU ops.** The biggest masked win came from reducing processing to affected regions instead of optimizing the existing full-frame loop (`masked-scale-roi`, overall gain with the masked instance dropping from 9.756s to 0.040s).
- **Dedicated CPU helpers are the strongest current lever when they remove generic mixed-mode plumbing for an exact hot preset family.** This repeatedly paid off for fixed no-mask shapes (`all-isophote-pde-fastpath` about +10.8% overall, `diffuse-watercolor-34-helper` about +1.0% overall with instance 2 +19.1%, `normal-first-fourth-cpu-helper` with instance 14 +6.6%, `normal-all-orders-isotropic-helper` with instance 18 +7.1%, `line-drawing-equal-orders-helper` with instance 1 +11.6%).
- **Matched-order fusion seems worthwhile only at the stencil-sum/input-combination level.** Precombining matched LF/HF stencil sums before convolution worked (`precombine-stencil-sums-for-matched-orders` +3.0%); deeper tensor/kernel fusion usually did not.
- **Use nontemporal stores only for true write-once, long-reuse-distance outputs.** This helped for HF detail writes and final output stores (`nontemporal-hf-writes-in-decomposition` +7.3%, `nontemporal-output-stores` +1.3%).
- **Keep the proven traversal shape and only peel the specific hot clamp-heavy PDE column path.** Column peeling helped (`peel-column-loop-to-eliminate-clamping` +2.4%); wider traversal rewrites generally regressed.
- **Exact convergence or provable-no-op removal can pay when it deletes repeated full passes, but only with a tight proof.** The best example is the coarse-iteration convergence stop, which improved overall runtime materially even though it missed the per-instance gate (`convergence-stop-coarse-iterations`, overall +3.3%).

## What to avoid
- **Do not scalarize the vector hot path or inline/replace `dt_vector_exp` with scalar logic.** Those changes produced the worst regressions by far (`inline-expf-evaluation-anisotropy` -170.9%, `inline-scalar-expf-fuse-all-pixel-passes` -114.4%, `explicit-scalarization-of-accumulation-arrays` -111.8%).
- **Do not collapse multiple hot `for_each_channel` phases into one giant fused pixel body.** Saved loop overhead was repeatedly dominated by register pressure and worse codegen (`pair-4-compute_convolution-calls-into-2-for_each_channel-loops` -48.7%, `phase-gradient-laplacian-into-scoped-blocks` -4.0%, `fuse-variance-regularization-into-loading-loop` -8.2%, `precompute-laplacian-and-axial-difference` -16.2%).
- **Do not disturb the established traversal and memory schedule.** Row-order rewrites, cache blocking, prefetching, precomputed index tables, border splitting in other dimensions, and LF/HF load interleaving consistently regressed (`remove-dwt-interleave-rows-to-restore-sequential-prefetching` -30.1%, `unswitch-row-bounds-for-static-offsets` -26.7%, `cache-block-pde-column-strips` -12.4%, `pde-interior-border-split` -6.2%, `prefetch-interleaved-row-neighbors` -6.0%, `interleave-lf-hf-neighbor-loads` -3.5%, `precompute-row-interleave-order` -3.0%, `pde-neighbor-index-tables` -3.3%).
- **Do not change store policy for buffers with short or medium reuse distance based on theory alone.** Wrong-way temporal/nontemporal changes regressed badly (`nontemporal-lf-writes-in-decomposition` -68.8%, `temporal-stores-for-intermediate-pde-scales` -6.0%, `normal-stores-intermediate-iteration-output` -0.3%).
- **Avoid outer-loop specialization or helper proliferation when it removes only a little work.** Code-size and scheduling costs usually outweighed the savings (`outer-loop-unswitch-matched-anisotropy` -17.1%, `outer-unswitch-zero-lf-contribution` -15.4%, `outer-unswitch-zero-sharpness` -13.1%, `unit-variance-fastpath` -6.4%, `outer-unswitch-has-mask` -3.5%, `reduce-dispatch-instantiations-16-to-9` -0.1%, `small-scale-bspline-dispatch` -0.2%, `first-fourth-two-order-body` -0.3%).
- **Avoid aggressive tensor/kernel/convolution fusion beyond the simple matched-stencil-sum case.** Broader attempts to merge tensors, kernels, or whole anisotropic accumulations were neutral to catastrophic (`fuse-matched-anisotropic-tensors` -7.1%, `algebraically-simplify-anisotropic-accumulation` -7.3%, `matched-order-kernel-reuse` -108.0%, `fuse-matched-gradient-and-laplacian-accumulations` -0.1%, `combine-isotropic-abcd-coefficients` -0.1%).
- **Avoid fast-math-style micro-ALU substitutions unless they also reduce live state or delete substantial work.** Reciprocal/division swaps, rsqrt, explicit FMA chains, and similar local rewrites were mostly noise or regressions (`replace-angle-divisions-with-inverse-magnitude` -2.6%, `rsqrt-anisotropic-magnitude` -0.8%, `explicit-fmaf-variance-chain` -2.2%, `extract-inverse-c2-subexpression` -4.4%, `precompute-directional-correction-factor` -2.2%, `precompute-reciprocal-variance` -3.1%).
- **Avoid mask-specific restructures unless they remove substantial whole-frame processing.** Span/row bookkeeping and sparse-init tweaks were neutral or negative; only ROI-style deletion of work paid (`mask-run-spans` -7.8%, `diffuse-mask-row-roi` -0.1%, `baseline-plus-delta` +0.2%, `sparse-inpaint-initialization` +0.5%, `skip-empty-coarse-mask-scales` +0.1%).
- **Do not expect allocation/layout/control-plane cleanups to move the benchmark much.** Buffer lifetime/layout/setup changes were mostly noise or regressions (`free-hf-buffers-after-reconstruction` -0.1%, `allocate-contiguous-hf-buffer-pool` -5.1%, `prebuild-scale-runtime-descriptors` -0.1%, `precompute-scale-parameters-outside-iteration-loop` -0.4%, `diffuse-scratch-buffer-aliasing` -0.3%).
- **Check applicability before implementing.** Several proposals were already present or structurally invalid in the current code, so the experiment would have been wasted: skip-exp-for-isotropic, direct neighbor fetching, derivative accumulation unrolling, dirty-region tracking, RGB-only hotpath, dilated-stencil sliding windows, and several zero-threshold/shared-conductance fast paths.
