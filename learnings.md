## What works
- **Eliminate hot-loop stack arrays and materialized intermediates.** This is still the biggest recurring win: direct source-pixel use and algebraic convolution beat staging arrays (`strip-internal-array-allocations-for-find_gradients-normalizations` +11.9%, `inline-symmetric-convolutions` +11.7%, `compound-near-miss-optimizations` +6.2%).
- **Prefer algebraic simplifications that reuse existing intermediates and reduce live state without changing traversal order.** Proven examples: deriving angular terms from squared derivatives, removing canceled `0.5f` factors, rewriting anisotropic accumulation as isotropic base plus directional correction, and directly inlining isotropic convolution (`compute-cos2-sin2-cos-sin-directly` +2.3%, `remove-0.5-scaling-from-gradient-laplacian-computation` +1.5%, `decompose-anisotropic-to-isotropic-base-and-correction` +1.7%, `inline-isotropic-convolutions` +1.2%).
- **Outer-loop specialization pays only when it removes expensive per-pixel math or skips whole passes.** Good cases deleted angle/sqrt/exp work or bypassed negligible scales entirely (`outer-loop-unswitch-isotropic-math` +6.4%, `skip-hf-decomposition-for-negligible-scales` +5.0%, `outer-unswitch-zero-speed-order-pairs` +2.8%, `skip-pde-negligible-norm` +2.0%).
- **Deleting whole-image or whole-scale work beats small inner-loop ALU tweaks.** The best masked result came from trimming work to the actual affected regions, not from mask bookkeeping changes (`masked-scale-roi` about +5.8%).
- **Preset-specific helpers are worthwhile only when they cut out a large block of generic mixed-mode plumbing.** The clear win is the no-mask all-isophote helper (`all-isophote-pde-fastpath` about +2.1%).
- **Fuse matched LF/HF work only at the stencil-sum/input-combination level.** Precombining matched stencil sums before convolution worked; broader tensor or kernel fusion did not (`precombine-stencil-sums-for-matched-orders` +3.0%).
- **Use nontemporal stores only for true write-once data with long reuse distance.** This helped for HF detail writes and final output writes (`nontemporal-hf-writes-in-decomposition` +7.3%, `nontemporal-output-stores` +1.3%).
- **Peel only the hot PDE column loop, preserving the existing row order and overall traversal.** That specific clamp-removal pattern paid off (`peel-column-loop-to-eliminate-clamping` +2.4%).

## What to avoid
- **Do not scalarize or inline the `dt_vector_exp` path, and do not force vector temporaries into scalar form.** Those are the worst regressions in the log (`inline-expf-evaluation-anisotropy` -170.9%, `inline-scalar-expf-fuse-all-pixel-passes` -114.4%, `explicit-scalarization-of-accumulation-arrays` -111.8%).
- **Do not fuse many hot `for_each_channel` phases into one giant pixel body, and do not create extra derived intermediates just to save a later loop.** The loop-overhead savings were consistently overwhelmed by register pressure and worse codegen (`pair-4-compute_convolution-calls-into-2-for_each_channel-loops` -48.7%, `phase-gradient-laplacian-into-scoped-blocks` -4.0%, `fuse-variance-regularization-into-loading-loop` -8.2%, `precompute-laplacian-and-axial-difference` -16.2%).
- **Do not disturb the established traversal and memory-scheduling pattern.** Row-order changes, cache blocking, prefetching, lookup tables, neighbor tables, and LF/HF load interleaving repeatedly regressed (`remove-dwt-interleave-rows-to-restore-sequential-prefetching` -30.1%, `unswitch-row-bounds-for-static-offsets` -26.7%, `cache-block-pde-column-strips` -12.4%, `prefetch-interleaved-row-neighbors` -6.0%, `interleave-lf-hf-neighbor-loads` -3.5%, `precompute-row-interleave-order` -3.0%, `pde-neighbor-index-tables` -3.3%).
- **Do not change store policy for short- or medium-reuse buffers based on theory alone.** Reuse distance dominated the outcome, and the wrong choice regressed badly (`nontemporal-lf-writes-in-decomposition` -68.8%, `temporal-stores-for-intermediate-pde-scales` -6.0%, `normal-stores-intermediate-iteration-output` -0.3%).
- **Avoid outer-loop specialization, helper proliferation, or dispatch changes when they remove only tiny work.** These usually add code size and scheduling cost without enough payoff (`outer-loop-unswitch-matched-anisotropy` -17.1%, `outer-unswitch-zero-lf-contribution` -15.4%, `outer-unswitch-zero-sharpness` -13.1%, `unit-variance-fastpath` -6.4%, `outer-unswitch-has-mask` -3.5%, `reduce-dispatch-instantiations-16-to-9` -0.1%, `small-scale-bspline-dispatch` -0.2%).
- **Avoid aggressive tensor/kernel/convolution fusion beyond the simple matched-stencil-sum case.** More ambitious attempts to merge tensors, reuse kernels, combine isotropic orders, or rewrite the accumulation algebra were neutral to catastrophic (`fuse-matched-anisotropic-tensors` -7.1%, `algebraically-simplify-anisotropic-accumulation` -7.3%, `matched-order-kernel-reuse` -108.0%, `fuse-matched-gradient-and-laplacian-accumulations` -0.1%, `combine-isotropic-abcd-coefficients` -0.1%).
- **Avoid fast-math-style micro-ALU rewrites unless they also reduce live state.** Reciprocal/division swaps, rsqrt paths, explicit FMA chains, and similar local substitutions were usually noise or regressions (`replace-angle-divisions-with-inverse-magnitude` -2.6%, `rsqrt-anisotropic-magnitude` -0.8%, `explicit-fmaf-variance-chain` -2.2%, `extract-inverse-c2-subexpression` -4.4%, `precompute-directional-correction-factor` -2.2%, `precompute-reciprocal-variance` -3.1%).
- **Avoid mask-specific restructures unless they delete substantial whole-frame work.** Spans, row classes, per-row intervals, split base-plus-delta passes, and sparse-init tweaks were neutral or negative once their control-flow overhead was included (`mask-run-spans` -7.8%, `diffuse-mask-row-roi` -0.1%, `baseline-plus-delta` +0.2%, `sparse-inpaint-initialization` +0.5%, `skip-empty-coarse-mask-scales` +0.1%).
- **Do not expect allocation/layout/control-plane tweaks to matter much.** Buffer lifetime changes, contiguous pools, prebuilt runtime descriptors, hoisted setup, and scratch aliasing were mostly noise or regressions (`free-hf-buffers-after-reconstruction` -0.1%, `allocate-contiguous-hf-buffer-pool` -5.1%, `prebuild-scale-runtime-descriptors` -0.1%, `precompute-scale-parameters-outside-iteration-loop` -0.4%, `diffuse-scratch-buffer-aliasing` -0.3%).
- **Verify applicability before coding.** Several ideas were already present or structurally invalid, so the experiment itself was wasted: skip-exp-for-isotropic, direct neighbor fetching, derivative accumulation unrolling, dirty-region tracking, RGB-only hotpath, and sliding-window neighbor reuse on dilated stencils were all already applied or not applicable.
