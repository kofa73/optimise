perf: express convolution via axial second derivatives to eliminate center weights

Currently, the anisotropic convolution evaluates `a22 * sum_tb + a11 * sum_lr - 2.0f * (a11 + a22) * center`. Mathematically, this is simply an expanded algebraic form of the second-order directional derivatives `a11 * D_xx + a22 * D_yy`. By precomputing the discrete axial second derivatives `D_yy = sum_tb - 2.0f * center` and `D_xx = sum_lr - 2.0f * center` once during the initial pixel gathering phase, we can completely eliminate the calculation of `a11 + a22` and its multiplications by the center pixel inside the heavily called `accumulate_convolution_direct` function. This elegantly simplifies the anisotropic accumulation to `abcd * (b11 * cross_corners + a22 * D_yy + a11 * D_xx)` and the isotropic accumulation to `abcd * (0.25f * sum_corners + 0.5f * (D_yy + D_xx) - center)`. This yields a strict net reduction in multiply-accumulate operations per channel and shortens the critical dependency path in the hot accumulator loop.
