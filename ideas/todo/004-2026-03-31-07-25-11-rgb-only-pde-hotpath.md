perf: split RGB diffusion from passthrough alpha in the CPU PDE loops

If the fourth channel is only carried through as opacity/alpha, keep it out of the gradient, variance, and convolution helpers and copy it only at stage boundaries. That reduces arithmetic and temporary-buffer traffic across the hottest loops by roughly a quarter for RGBA images, and it can be implemented cleanly with shared 3-channel inline helpers plus a simple passthrough path for channel 3.
